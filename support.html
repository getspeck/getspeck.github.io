Support

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, write to us.

Email hello@getspeck.app. We usually reply within two days. Speck accounts have no name or email, so tell us roughly when you bought or released something and we'll find it.

Blank specks

What is a blank speck?

Each blank speck holds one line of thought, up to 100 characters. A thought can use up to 8 specks. Blank specks never expire.

I bought specks in the app but they didn't arrive.

They usually appear within a few seconds. If not, close and reopen Speck while you're online. If they're still missing after a few minutes, email us with the date and pack size.

Can I give specks to someone?

Yes. In the app, open You → Give blank specks to create a gift code, or open a thought that moved you and send specks to its author. Gifts come from your own blank specks, and an unopened gift code comes back to you after 30 days.

Buying on the website

How do specks bought on getspeck.app reach the app?

After you pay, we email you a link. Open it on your iPhone and Speck adds the purchase to your Sky. Specks bought before January 1, 2027 are reserved for launch: open the link in Speck on January 1.

My link expired.

Links last an hour. Open it anyway: Speck will send a fresh one to the same email.

Speck Plus

What do members get?

70 blank specks every month and more Distills. Founding Members who join before launch keep their number for as long as they stay members.

How do I cancel?

  • Joined in the app: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Speck.
  • Joined on the website: use the link in your receipt email, or email us.

You keep Plus until the end of the period you've paid for.

Refunds

  • In-app purchases are refunded by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Website purchases: email us. Packs reserved before January 1, 2027 are fully refundable until then.

Specks from a refunded purchase are removed, as far as they haven't been used.

Your account

How do I keep my Sky on a new iPhone?

In Speck, open You and connect Sign in with Apple. On your new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple ID. Without it, your account lives only on this iPhone.

Can I edit or delete a thought?

You have 60 seconds after releasing to reclaim it. After that it's permanent: no one can edit or delete it, including us. That's the promise of the Sky.

How do I delete my account?

Open You → Delete account. Your thoughts stay in the Sky, with nothing connecting them to you. Unused blank specks are lost. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Safety

How do I report a thought?

Open the thought and tap the flag. Reports are reviewed by people who never see who wrote it. Thoughts that break the rules of the Sky are hidden.

If you're struggling

Speck isn't a crisis service. If you're in danger, contact local emergency services. In the U.S., call or text 988 any time.
