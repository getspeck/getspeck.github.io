What Speck is

Every thought is a speck.
Every speck becomes part of something bigger.

Social media is filled with videos, images, reactions, and endless noise. But sometimes, a few words are all you need.

A thought doesn’t have to change the world to mean something. It can be funny, personal, strange, beautiful, unfinished, or something you’ve never said out loud. Here, your words become part of a universe built by everyone.

No profiles to maintain. No followers to chase. No likes to compete for. Just thoughts.

The Rehearsal Sky and the Great Reset

The beginning of something bigger.

  1. Now

    Explore the Sky

    Explore the Sky before Genesis. Release a demo thought, choose its color, and discover what others have left behind, on the web and on iPhone, live. The Rehearsal Sky is temporary. Demo thoughts disappear at the Great Reset and never enter the permanent Sky. Demo the Sky

  2. December 31, 2026 · 11:59 p.m. EST

    The Great Reset

    All demo and rehearsal thoughts are cleared. The Sky returns to darkness, ready for Genesis.

  3. January 1, 2027 · 12:00 a.m. EST

    Genesis · 001 awaiting

    Midnight in New York. One beginning, everywhere. The permanent Sky begins, and the first real thought starts its history. Once released, your words can’t be edited. Write something you’re ready to leave behind.

  4. Planned

    Constellations

    Connect your thoughts. Individual specks can be connected into larger collections: build a narrative, preserve a series of ideas, or connect thoughts that belong together. A single thought can stand alone. Connected thoughts can tell a story.

  5. Planned for spring/summer 2027

    Nebulas

    Shared skies for communities, topics, and questions. Host a Nebula, invite others to contribute, and discover what takes shape when thoughts come together.

The universe will keep growing. Planned features and release dates are subject to change.
