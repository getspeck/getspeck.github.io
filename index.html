What Speck is

Every light is someone’s thought.

Write what’s on your mind, up to 100 characters at a time, and release it. It becomes a light in a sky shared by everyone, drifting further away the longer it’s been there.

No names. No followers. No likes to chase. Someone might feel what you wrote, and neither of you will ever know who the other is.
